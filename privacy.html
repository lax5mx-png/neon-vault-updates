Neon Vault — Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Neon Vault is a casino-themed game played with virtual credits. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

No real money

Credits, keys and skins in Neon Vault are virtual, have no real-world value, and cannot be bought, sold, cashed out or exchanged for money or prizes. The app has no in-app purchases.

Information the game stores

Your progress (credits, skins, stats and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive your game data, and we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. You don't need an account to play.

Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your own Google account if you have device backup turned on. If you use the in-game Backup feature, the backup file is saved where you choose and is not sent to us.

Optional reward ads

The app can show a video ad only when you choose to watch one (for example, to skip a job's recharge timer). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects information such as your IP address (which can indicate your general location), interactions with ads and the app, diagnostic information, and device identifiers like the Android advertising ID. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before ads use your data, and you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Update checks

Copies of the app installed from Google Play are updated by Google Play. Copies installed directly from the developer's download page check that page for new versions; this sends a normal web request and no personal information.

Children

Neon Vault contains simulated gambling and is intended for players 18 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Because your game data stays on your device, you can delete all of it by using Settings → Reset all progress in the game, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions, contact the developer using the email address shown on the Neon Vault Google Play listing.
